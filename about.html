About Best Kitchen Find

A small, ad-free food site built around cooking that gets people to the table.

A magazine about one great find

BestKitchenFind is a cooking magazine for people who love feeding others. We write for the home cook planning a Saturday cookout, the parent flipping breakfast for a crowd and the gift buyer hunting for something that will actually get used.

Most kitchen sites try to cover every gadget on the market. We went the other way. After years of cooking over charcoal, we kept coming back to one piece of gear: a flat-pack carbon steel grill that slides together by hand, holds a cast-iron pan and packs thin enough to live in a closet. That grill is the Slot Grill, and it is the find this site is named for.

What you will find here

  • Recipes for the grill, the griddle and cast iron over coals.
  • Technique guides on fire building, searing, smoke and temperatures.
  • Carbon steel care from seasoning to rust rescue.
  • Cookout and entertaining plans for backyards, tailgates and parks.
  • Gift guides for holidays, dads, couples and small budgets.
  • Pantry recipes for rubs, marinades, brines, sauces and butters.

Our rules

  • We feature one product line and explain it in depth.
  • Every product fact on this site is checked against the maker's own listings. If a spec is not published, we do not guess.
  • Food safety temperatures follow USDA guidance.
  • No ads and no sponsored content.

Who is behind it

The site is run by Keith, a home cook and charcoal enthusiast who believes the best meals are cooked outdoors and shared with friends. Questions, recipe ideas and cookout stories are always welcome.
